Execution Framework 04 Stages

How we deliver Patching as a Service.

01 — SCAN

Vulnerability & Asset Discovery

We scan all servers and endpoints to identify missing OS patches, outdated apps, and active CVEs.

02 — STAGE

Ring Grouping & Snapshots

Assets are grouped into Test, Pilot, and Production rings with automated pre-patch backup policies.

03 — DEPLOY

Off-Hours Automated Rollout

Approved patches deploy during agreed maintenance windows with live engineering supervision.

04 — VERIFY

Post-Patch Health & Audit Report

We verify application health post-reboot and deliver a clean compliance report after every cycle.
